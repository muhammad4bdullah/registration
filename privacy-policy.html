Privacy

Privacy Policy

AMUN is committed to protecting the personal information of delegates, guardians and participants. This page will be finalized before public launch.

Information we may collect

  • Name, email address, phone number and guardian contact details.
  • Registration and committee preference information.
  • Proof of payment and relevant event records.

How we use it

Information is used to process delegate registration, maintain event records, contact participants, and ensure smooth operations for the conference.

Data protection

We will handle personal data responsibly and store it securely using approved systems. Only necessary information will be accessed by authorized AMUN organizers.

Consent

By submitting a registration form, participants consent to the processing of information necessary for delegate registration and conference administration.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated before launch and after the event begins. The latest version will remain available on this page.
