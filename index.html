About AMUN

A student conference built around diplomacy.

Model United Nations gives students the opportunity to represent countries, research international issues and participate in structured diplomatic debate.

At AMUN, delegates are encouraged to listen, negotiate and work with others while developing confidence in public speaking.

Committees

Choose your committee.

Explore the committees available at AMUN. Click a committee to open its details.

COMMITTEE 01

UNHRC

United Nations Human Rights Council
↗

Delegates represent countries and examine how governments and international institutions can protect human rights.

Expect evidence-based speeches, moderated debate, negotiation and collaborative work on resolutions. This committee is a good fit for delegates interested in humanitarian issues and diplomacy.

COMMITTEE 02

UNSC

United Nations Security Council
↗

The Security Council focuses on international peace and security, with delegates balancing national interests and the need for collective action.

Sessions involve formal debate, negotiation and drafting practical responses to the issue under discussion. Delegates should be ready to build alliances and defend their country's position.

COMMITTEE 03

Crisis Committee

↗

Crisis delegates respond to a developing scenario as new information and events change the situation.

In addition to debate, delegates work through updates, negotiate with other participants and propose timely actions. Adaptability, teamwork and clear decision-making are especially useful here.

COMMITTEE 04

ICJ

International Court of Justice
↗

The International Court of Justice considers legal disputes between states and questions of international law.

Participants take part in structured legal proceedings, presenting arguments and examining the positions of the parties. Careful research, logical reasoning and respectful advocacy are central to this committee.

COMMITTEE 05

UNW

UN Women
↗

UN Women focuses on gender equality and the empowerment of women and girls around the world.

Delegates examine barriers to equal participation, explore policy responses and negotiate practical solutions. Research, inclusive discussion and collaborative resolution-writing are central to this committee.
