APSACS MUN - Participant Eligibility Criteria

  1. Age Limit: Participants must be between 12 - 20 years of age. Age will be verified via B-Form / School ID at registration.
  2. Class / Grade Requirement: Open for students of Class VI to Class XII only. Students below Class VI and above Class XII are not eligible.
  3. Gender: Both male and female students can participate. We encourage equal participation from all.
  4. Institution: Open to students from schools and colleges. Individual / private delegates are allowed with valid student proof.
  5. General Rules: Participants must have a valid school ID card, be available for all conference days, maintain discipline and MUN decorum, and note that registration fees are non-refundable.

AMUN policies

AMUN is committed to protecting the personal information of delegates, guardians and participants. This page will be finalized before public launch.

Information we may collect

  • Name, email address, phone number and guardian contact details.

How we use it

Information is used to process delegate registration, maintain event records, contact participants, and ensure smooth operations for the conference.

Data protection
